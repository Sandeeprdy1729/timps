The problem

Your AI agents are born with amnesia.

Session ends, context dies. You re-explain your architecture, your bug patterns, your preferences — over and over again.

🧠

Encoding failure

Context is discarded the moment a session ends. If it never reaches long-term storage, it never happened.

🔍

Retrieval failure

Even when context is saved, agents lack the cue-based systems to surface the right memory at the right time.

📉

Decay

Without rehearsal and consolidation, memories fade. The Ebbinghaus curve applies to AI too.

💥

Reconsolidation

Every edit to a stored memory risks losing the original truth. Most systems silently overwrite.

How it works

Three quiet steps.

01 / SYNC

Connect

One click hands off to TIMPS running on your machine. Real OAuth, read-only scope. Tokens stay local.

02 / DISTILL

Convert

Each email becomes 1–3 knowledge facts. Raw mail never leaves your store.

03 / REMEMBER

Recall

Facts answer timps recall from any tool, and show up in the desktop app and memory graph.

Cognitive architecture

Five layers. Complete understanding.

Most memory solutions are a vector DB. TIMPS is a cognitive architecture — five working systems, like human memory.
