How it works

Three quiet steps.

01 / SYNC

Connect

One click hands off to TIMPS on your machine. Real OAuth, read-only scope.

02 / DISTILL

Convert

Each email becomes 1–3 knowledge facts. Raw mail never leaves the store.

03 / REMEMBER

Recall

Facts answer timps recall, appear in the dashboard, and feed the live memory page.
